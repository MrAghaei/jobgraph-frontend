# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Iranian job seekers (software engineers and tech professionals) who are actively looking for work and tired of checking multiple job boards. They want one place to discover roles, compare opportunities, and understand market demand before they apply. The marketing site and the product app serve the same person at different moments: discovery and conversion on the site, search and decision-making in the app. Both surfaces carry equal weight.

## Product Purpose

Jobgraph aggregates real-time tech job listings in one place for Iranian seekers, so they stop bouncing between websites. It normalizes scraped postings into structured data (titles, companies, experience level, and keyword tags) and pairs that with market analytics so users can decide faster. Premium users get instant alerts when matching roles appear (Telegram webhooks and email digests). Success means less time scrolling and more qualified applications backed by up-to-date market context.

## Positioning

Real-time tech job discovery + market intelligence + instant alerts for Iranian engineers.

## Brand Personality

Sharp and modern. Tool-first, precise, built for people who value their time. The feel should be opinionated and fast — closer to Arc or Raycast than a traditional job board. Direct language, minimal chrome, confident data presentation. Not warm-and-fuzzy, not corporate, not playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: cream backgrounds, gradient heroes, "AI-powered" eyebrows on every section
- Classic job board clutter: dense ads, noisy filters, endless list fatigue (Indeed, old LinkedIn jobs)
- Corporate enterprise UI: gray tables, stock photos, synergy energy
- Overly playful startup aesthetics: mascots, bouncy motion, cartoon illustrations

## Design Principles

1. **Respect time** — Every screen should reduce friction. If a flow adds steps without adding clarity, cut it.
2. **One place, one flow** — Discover, filter, compare, and decide without leaving Jobgraph or opening another tab.
3. **Data drives decisions** — Structured job data and market analytics aren't decoration; they should change what the user applies to and when.
4. **Momentum via alerts** — Premium users should be notified quickly enough to act while a role is still fresh.
5. **Sharp over soft** — Tool-like precision beats comforting filler. Clarity is the kindness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target yet. Follow best practices from the start: sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic markup, and reduced-motion alternatives for any animation. The UI must support RTL as the default direction for Iranian users (including mirrored layout, correct text alignment, and appropriate `dir` / `lang` metadata).
